16 note alert
How to play^L^L
A move places one of your discs so that a straight line of Lisa's
discs -- across, up and down, or diagonal -- is caught between it and
another of yours.  Those discs turn over to your color.  Every move
must turn at least one disc; the dots on the board show where you can
play.  If you have no move your turn is skipped.^L^L
The game ends when neither side can move.  The most discs wins.
Corners are worth having: a corner disc can never be turned.^L^L
Black moves first.  You are black unless you choose Switch Sides in
the Game menu, which starts a new game with Lisa opening as black.
